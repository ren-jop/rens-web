#Ren's website

This is a personal website that I have been making. I was originally inspired by my favourite content creators with their websites being reysu.io, jkwon.co and jvscholz.com. Which all have their personal taste which was something I wanted to include.

But I did go for a very minimalistic look but I think that it is powerful to have minimalistic qualities in such an overbearing world. My favourite part about this site is the photo that I took on the front screen.

I do aim to add more features in the future though despite the minimalistic focus so that I can showcase my projects and also make a blog or something which would be cool. But apart from that I think that showing it to workplaces that I am interested in attending would even be a step in the next direction because they could be pleased with the aesthetic and qualities I aimed for. I also would like to add a banner at the top and a footprint or thing to put at the bottom.
